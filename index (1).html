Key insights

  • Lunch (12–1 pm) and dinner (7–9 pm) carry about 44% of orders, and delivery is about 35 min then vs about 29 min off-peak.
  • Orders beyond 8 km average about 57 min, are rated 3.5 and have the highest cancellation rate (6.2%).
  • Rating falls from 4.79 (under 25 min) to 3.25 (over 55 min).
  • About 54% of customers reorder; the top 10% of customers give about 44% of revenue.
  • Revenue dipped from October to December (-1.0%, then -5.3% month on month).
